## What's .Yo?

**.sup** captures content from any app — conversations, code, emails, docs — and indexes it locally with vector embeddings.

**.yo** searches across everything you've captured using natural language. An AI agent finds what you need and formats results using skills.

**.dip** and **.drip** are visual effects and automation commands that chain actions across your panels.

All data stays local in `~/yo/store/`. Your content, your machine, your control.
